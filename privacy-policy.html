Privacy Policy — Splat That!

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Splat That! ("the game") is made by Chloe Hewer. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (Splat Points, upgrades, scores, settings, missions and achievements) is saved on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. The optional player names you type in the game stay on your device. Several people can have their own player on one device (for example a parent and a child); each player's progress and year of birth are stored separately on the device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where required by law (for example in the EU/UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile name, player ID and avatar are used to show the world leaderboard and achievements. Your best round (with your score from that game) is submitted to Google's leaderboard and is visible to other players.

If you choose to, you can let the game see your Google Play Games friends list. Google asks you first, and you can say no. It is only used to show a leaderboard of your friends inside the game, and to let you find and add friends using Google's own screens. We do not store your friends list or share it with anyone, and you can turn this off at any time in your Google Play Games settings.

While you are signed in, a copy of your game progress is also kept in your Google Play Games saved games, so it can be restored on a new phone. You can delete it from your Google Play Games settings. Google Play Games is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is designed for players of all ages. When it is first opened it asks for the player's year of birth, which is stored on the device only and used to choose the right settings. For players under 13: ads are requested as child-directed and are never personalised, ads are limited to content rated for general audiences, and the online leaderboard and Google Play Games sign-in are turned off. Players under 18 never receive personalised ads. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are made through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases using Google Family Link. If you are a parent and have questions, contact us at the address below.

Changes
